# Figure archive

Images generated during the project, kept so that each stage can be
inspected without re-running it. Dates refer to `NOTEBOOK.md` entries.
Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`. Figures 08-15 are regenerated by
`scripts/make_figures.py` from the `work/` runs; the earlier ones were
produced inline during the corresponding steps and are archived as made.

Attribution: DISR images are from the ESA Planetary Science Archive
(HP-SSA-DISR-2-3-EDR-RDR-V1.3), processed by E. Karkoschka (G-images).
Figures 07b, 07c, 07d and 12 show the IPGP DTM/orthomosaic: Daudon et al.
(2020), Earth and Space Science, doi:10.1029/2020EA001127, dataset
IPGP_Titan_Huygens_V1.0 (ESA PSA).
